**Verified 2026-11-22.** The list has **39 entries**; 30 carry a DOI and were checked
against Crossref (authors, year, title, journal, volume, first page). Nine entries
have no DOI — books, an agency report and the unindexed AGSO Journal 17(2) 1997
issue — and are kept as manual entries.

## Style

APA 7, as the journal requires: `Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C.
(Year). Title in sentence case. *Journal Name, Volume*(Issue), pages.
https://doi.org/...` Journal and book titles are italicised in the Word file.
In-text citations are `(Author, Year)`, `(Author & Author, Year)` for two authors,
`(Author et al., Year)` for three or more; narrative citations use "and".

## Citation coverage

Every entry is cited in the body and every in-text citation has an entry:
56 citation instances, no orphans in either direction, no duplicate entries.
Names with several works are distinguished by year (Zhang et al., 2024; Zhang et
al., 2026).

## What to check at proof

1. The Bar-Shalom year, if the copy-editor prefers the Crossref date.
2. The Hook et al. page range.
3. That the italic formatting survived the journal's typesetting.

Do not add new references without a DOI unless they are books, agency reports or
the AGSO 1997 issue; everything else should be Crossref-verifiable.
